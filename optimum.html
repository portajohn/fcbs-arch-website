Optimum

Custom steel, aluminum, bronze, and stainless windows and doors — since 1985

A Heritage of Precision Steel

Founded in 1985 in upstate New York, Optimum Window has grown into the largest and most diversified manufacturer of steel windows and doors in the United States. Every unit is built to order from the architect's shop drawings — fire-rated and non-rated steel, aluminum, bronze alloy, and stainless — for commercial, high-end residential, and landmark applications.

Their workshop pairs traditional steel craft with modern CNC fabrication, and a sustainability program built around 100% recycled steel. The result is a product line that holds its own on a Manhattan landmark renovation, a Carolina coastal home, or anywhere a slim, durable steel profile is the right answer.
