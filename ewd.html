Exclusive Wood Doors

Bespoke solid-wood entry doors, built to signature specification

The Art of the Entrance

Exclusive Wood Doors builds entry doors the old way — one at a time, to a single home's exact specification. Each door is a study in solid timber: hand-selected species, precision joinery, and finishes developed to stand up to coastal sun and salt air without losing the warmth that only real wood delivers.

From towering pivot entries to arched double doors and custom lite patterns, no design is off the table. We work from your drawings — or a reference image and a conversation — to engineer a door that arrives ready to hang, hardware-prepped and weather-sealed, as the centerpiece of the facade.
