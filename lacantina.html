LaCantina

Large openings and indoor–outdoor living, perfected since 1981

Open Up the Space

Founded in 1981 in Oceanside, California, LaCantina has spent four decades perfecting the door systems that dissolve the line between inside and out. They engineered the modern folding door — the industry has been catching up ever since.

The full collection spans folding, multi-slide, pocket, and swing systems in aluminum, wood, and vinyl. Their flagship V2 folding door pushes the format further with ultra-thin stiles and edge-to-edge glass — maximum opening, minimum frame.
